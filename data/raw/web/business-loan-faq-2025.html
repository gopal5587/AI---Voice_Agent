Frequently Asked Questions - Business Loan

Last updated: Nov 2, 2025

What documents do I need to apply?

You need PAN and Aadhaar for KYC, the last 12 months of business bank statements, GST returns or ITR for the last 2 years, and business registration proof such as GST certificate, Udyam registration or shop and establishment licence.

Can I prepay or foreclose my loan?

Yes. You can foreclose the loan after paying 12 EMIs. A foreclosure charge of 5% of the outstanding principal applies.

What happens if I miss an EMI?

A late payment charge of 2% per month is levied on the overdue instalment amount, plus a cheque or NACH bounce charge of Rs. 500 per instance. Missed monthly instalments are reported to credit bureaus and can lower your credit score.

How can I speak to a person?

A relationship manager can call you back within 1 business day. Callbacks are available Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST.
