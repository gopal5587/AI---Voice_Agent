Kosha Business Growth Loan

Grow your business without pledging collateral. Apply in minutes!

Loan Amount and Tenure

The Kosha Business Growth Loan is an unsecured term loan for MSMEs, proprietors, partnership firms and private limited companies. Loan amounts range from Rs. 1,00,000 (1 lakh) to Rs. 50,00,000 (50 lakh). Repayment tenure ranges from 12 to 60 months, repaid through fixed monthly instalments.

Interest Rate

Interest rates range from 14% to 24% per annum on a reducing-balance basis. The final rate is risk-based and depends on business vintage, turnover, credit score and banking history. The exact rate, all fees and the total cost of the loan are shown in the Key Fact Statement (KFS) before you sign.

No Collateral Required

The Business Growth Loan does not require property, gold or any other collateral. A personal guarantee from the promoter or proprietor is required.

Uses of the Loan

Funds can be used for working capital, inventory purchase, equipment purchase, business expansion, and renovation of business premises. Funds cannot be used for speculative real estate, stock market trading, cryptocurrency trading, gambling, or repaying personal credit card debt.

Disbursal Timeline

After approval and signing of the loan agreement, disbursal typically happens within 3 working days. Approval is subject to credit assessment and is never guaranteed.
