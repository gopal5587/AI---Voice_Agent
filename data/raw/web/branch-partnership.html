Branch Partnership Program

Branch Partnership Benefits

Operational, marketing, and technology support is provided to branch partners. Partners receive co-branded marketing material, access to the Kosha partner portal for lead tracking, and training for their staff.

Partner Commission

Branch partners earn a payout of 1% to 1.5% of the disbursed loan amount for every referred business loan that is disbursed. Payouts are settled monthly.

Permitted Uses for Referred Loans

Funds can be used for working capital, inventory purchases, equipment purchase, business expansion, and renovation of business premises. Funds cannot be used for speculative real estate, stock market trading, cryptocurrency trading, gambling, or repaying personal credit card debt.

Who Can Become a Partner

Chartered accountants, DSAs, business correspondents and established local financial service firms with at least 2 years of operating history can apply to become branch partners.
