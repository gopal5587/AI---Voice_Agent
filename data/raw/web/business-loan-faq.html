Frequently Asked Questions - Business Loan

Last updated: 15/07/2026

What documents do I need to apply?

You need PAN and Aadhaar for KYC, the last 12 months of business bank statements, GST returns or ITR for the last 2 years, and business registration proof such as GST certificate, Udyam registration or shop and establishment licence.

Can I prepay or foreclose my loan?

Yes. You can foreclose the loan after paying 6 EMIs. A foreclosure charge of 4% of the outstanding principal applies. Part-prepayment is allowed once a year up to 25% of the outstanding principal with a 2% charge on the prepaid amount.

What happens if I miss an EMI?

A late payment charge of 2% per month is levied on the overdue instalment amount, plus a cheque or NACH bounce charge of Rs. 500 per instance. Missed monthly instalments are reported to credit bureaus and can lower your credit score.

How is my data used?

Your documents are used only for KYC verification and credit assessment, stored in encrypted form, and shared only with credit bureaus and regulators as required by law. We never sell customer data.

Are there any hidden charges?

No. Every applicable charge, including the processing fee, interest, foreclosure charge and late payment charge, is listed in the Key Fact Statement shared before you sign. The processing fee is up to 2% of the loan amount plus GST and is deducted from the disbursed amount.

I already have a loan with a bank. Can I still apply?

Yes. An existing loan does not automatically disqualify you. Your existing EMIs are considered when assessing repayment capacity. Kosha Capital does not offer balance transfer or debt consolidation for business loans.

How can I speak to a person?

A relationship manager can call you back within 1 business day. Callbacks are available Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST. You can also email support@kosha-demo.example.

Uses of the Loan

Funds can be used for working capital, inventory purchase, equipment purchase, business expansion, and renovation of business premises. Funds cannot be used for speculative real estate, stock market trading, cryptocurrency trading, gambling, or repaying personal credit card debt.
