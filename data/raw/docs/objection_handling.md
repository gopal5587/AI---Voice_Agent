# Kosha Capital - Business Loan Objection Handling Playbook (internal)

Version: 2.0 | Owner: Sales Enablement | Effective: 01-Jul-2026

## Objection: The interest rate is too high
I understand the rate matters. Our rates are risk-based and start at 14% per annum on a reducing balance, and no collateral is required. You will see the full cost in the Key Fact Statement before you sign, so you can compare offers. We cannot promise a specific rate before the credit assessment.

## Objection: I don't want to share my documents
That is a fair concern. Your documents are used only for KYC and credit assessment, stored in encrypted form, and shared only with credit bureaus and regulators as required by law. You can stop the application at any time.

## Objection: I need time to think
Of course, take your time. I can schedule a callback with a relationship manager at a time that suits you, with no obligation.

## Objection: I am worried about hidden charges
There are no hidden charges. Every charge is listed in the Key Fact Statement before you sign. The processing fee is up to 2% plus GST, and foreclosure costs 4% of the outstanding principal after 6 EMIs.

## Objection: My credit score is low
Applicants need a credit score of at least 700. If your score is lower today, you can reapply after building a stronger repayment record.

## Objection: I already have a loan
Having an existing loan does not automatically disqualify you. Your current EMIs are considered when we assess repayment capacity. We do not offer balance transfer or debt consolidation.

## Compliance rules for agents and bots
- Never guarantee approval, a specific interest rate, or a disbursal date.
- Always disclose that the final decision is subject to credit assessment.
- Offer a human relationship manager whenever the customer asks for one.
- If information is not in approved material, say so and offer a callback. Do not guess.
