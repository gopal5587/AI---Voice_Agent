Lumina Protect Plus - Term Life Insurance

Coverage

Lumina Protect Plus is a term life insurance plan offered through Bangko Kapwa branches (bancassurance). Sum assured (coverage) ranges from PHP 500,000 to PHP 5,000,000. Entry age is 18 to 60 years old, and coverage continues until age 70.

Sample Premium

For a 30-year-old non-smoker, coverage of PHP 1,000,000 costs about PHP 1,250 per month. This is an illustration only; the final premium depends on age, health declaration and underwriting.

Payment Modes

Premiums can be paid monthly, quarterly, semi-annually or annually through auto-debit from a Bangko Kapwa savings account, over the counter at any Bangko Kapwa branch, or through the Lumina app.

Riders

Optional riders: Accidental Death Benefit (doubles the payout for accidental death), Critical Illness rider (advance payout of up to 50% of the sum assured upon diagnosis of a covered critical illness), and Waiver of Premium (future premiums are waived if the policyholder becomes totally and permanently disabled).

Beneficiaries

You can name up to 5 beneficiaries. Beneficiaries can be revocable (you may change them anytime) or irrevocable (changes require the beneficiary's written consent).

Grace Period and Lapse

There is a 31-day grace period after the premium due date. If the premium is still unpaid after the grace period, the policy lapses and coverage stops. A lapsed policy can be reinstated within 2 years by paying the back premiums and submitting a health declaration.

Free Look Period

You have 15 days from receipt of the policy to cancel it for a full refund of premiums paid, minus medical exam costs if any.

Lumina Life Philippines and Bangko Kapwa are fictional companies for an assessment (DEMO). Regulated-entity notices are illustrative only.
