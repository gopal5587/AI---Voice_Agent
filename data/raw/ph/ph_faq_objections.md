# Lumina Life (DEMO) - Bank Referral Call Guide and FAQ

Market: Philippines | Channel: Bangko Kapwa bank referral | Version: 1.1 | Effective: 2026-08-01

## FAQ: Do I need a medical exam?
For coverage up to PHP 2,000,000 at age 45 and below, a health declaration is usually enough. Higher coverage or older applicants may need a medical exam at an accredited clinic, and Lumina Life pays for it.

## FAQ: How are claims paid?
Beneficiaries submit the claim form, death certificate and valid IDs at any Bangko Kapwa branch. Approved claims are paid within 10 working days after complete documents.

## FAQ: Contestability period
For the first 2 years, Lumina Life may review a claim if information in the application was misrepresented. After 2 years the policy becomes incontestable except for fraud.

## Objection: Mahal / too expensive
Naiintindihan ko po, budget is important. Coverage can start at PHP 500,000 and the premium can be paid monthly through auto-debit. You can start with a lower sum assured and add riders later.

## Objection: I already have an HMO
An HMO pays hospital and medical bills while you are alive. Life insurance gives a cash benefit to your beneficiaries if you pass away. They serve different needs and work well together.

## Objection: Is this a scam? / Trust concern
The policy is issued by Lumina Life and sold only by licensed financial advisors at Bangko Kapwa. You can verify the advisor's license and visit any branch. You will never be asked to send money to a personal account.

## Objection: I'm still young, I don't need it yet
Premiums are lower while you are young and healthy, and the rate is locked for the term. Coverage also protects the people who depend on you, like your parents, spouse or children.

## Objection: I need to think about it / Pag-iisipan ko muna
Walang problema po, take your time. A licensed financial advisor can call you back at a time convenient for you.

## Compliance rules
- The bot is not a licensed financial advisor and must say so if asked for personal financial advice.
- Premium amounts are illustrations; the final premium is subject to underwriting.
- Never say a claim is guaranteed to be paid.
- Offer a licensed financial advisor whenever the customer asks for a person.
